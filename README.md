## 💍 Pages & Core Features

### 1. Home (`index.html`)
- Full-screen cinematic hero with headline: *"Your Dream Wedding, Perfectly Planned"*
- Dual CTAs: *"Plan Your Wedding"* & *"View Our Work"*
- 4-milestone credibility bar (250+ Weddings, 14+ Cities, 100% Happy Couples, 8+ Years)
- Short company introduction with core promises
- Signature services preview (4 elegant cards)
- Featured weddings (4 real couples' stories: Udaipur, Goa, Nashik, Mumbai)
- Interactive real couple testimonials slider with auto-play & dot pagination
- Emotional call-to-action banner

### 2. Our Services (`services.html`)
- 6 comprehensive wedding services:
  1. Full Wedding Planning
  2. Partial Wedding Planning
  3. Venue Selection & Management
  4. Décor & Styling
  5. Destination Weddings
  6. Wedding Day Coordination
- Each service features starting prices, detailed inclusion bullets, and direct *"Inquire Now"* links
- The 4-Step Curated Journey timeline
- Interactive FAQ accordions

### 3. Portfolio (`portfolio.html`)
- Filterable gallery: *All*, *Traditional & Royal*, *Destination Escapes*, *Intimate & Vineyard*, *Grand Ballrooms*
- 16 high-resolution romantic wedding photographs from real celebrations
- **Working Lightbox Modal**: Click-to-zoom, couple names, venue, next/previous buttons, and keyboard navigation (*Left/Right Arrow*, *Escape*)

### 4. Packages (`packages.html`)
- 3 clear investment tiers:
  - **Essential Package**: Starting from ₹1,50,000 (intimate weddings & coordination)
  - **Premium Package** (Most Popular): Starting from ₹3,50,000 (complete 360° management)
  - **Luxury Package**: Starting from ₹7,00,000 (royal destination extravaganza)
- Side-by-side comprehensive feature comparison table
- *"Get Quote"* buttons that automatically pre-select the package in the inquiry form

### 5. About Us (`about.html`)
- Origin story and emotional philosophy
- Founder’s note & photo (*Ananya Malhotra*, Founder & Creative Director)
- Core team profiles with roles and bios (Production, Floral Design, Hospitality)
- Key achievements & industry recognitions (*Vogue Weddings*, *WedMeGood*, *WeddingSutra*, *Elle Decor*)
- The 4 Pillars of Excellence

### 6. Inquire Now (`inquire.html`)
- Interactive inquiry form with real-time validation:
  - Full Name (required)
  - Phone Number (required, 10+ digits validation)
  - Email (required, regex validation)
  - Wedding Date
  - Preferred Venue / City
  - Estimated Guest Count
  - Service / Package Type (auto-selected from URL query parameters)
  - Message / Special Notes
- On successful submission:
  - Displays an elegant confirmation modal: *"Thank you for reaching out! We will contact you within 24 hours."*
  - Provides a unique booking reference code and a direct button to confirm instantaneously on WhatsApp
- Direct Call and WhatsApp quick-contact options
- Design studio address and visiting hours
