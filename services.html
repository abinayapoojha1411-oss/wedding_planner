Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to the most common queries our couples have when planning their wedding with us.

How far in advance should we book your services?

We recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance, especially for peak wedding dates (October to March) and high-demand destination venues in Udaipur, Jaipur, and Goa. However, we also accommodate short-notice weddings through our expedited planning service.

Do you work with our pre-selected vendors?

Absolutely! While we have a curated roster of India’s top photographers, caterers, makeup artists, and decorators, we are thrilled to collaborate with any vendors you have already fallen in love with.

Can you plan weddings outside of India?

Yes. Our destination team has orchestrated international celebrations in Dubai, Thailand, Italy (Lake Como), and Bali. We manage international flight blocks, bilingual logistics, overseas artist riders, and customs documentation.

How are payments and deposits structured?

We operate on an easy milestone-based structure: an initial booking retainer to lock your dates, followed by scheduled installments aligned with vendor booking deadlines, and a final settlement prior to wedding week.

Ready for an Unforgettable Celebration?

Tell us about your dream vision, favorite locations, and estimated guest count. Let our specialists craft a personalized roadmap.
