Born From Passion

Turning Sacred Vows into Living Art

Eternal Moments Weddings was founded in 2018 with a singular, resolute vision: to liberate couples and their families from the exhausting chaos of event planning, allowing them to truly feel, breathe, and revel in the emotional sanctity of their wedding day.

Over the past 8 years, our Mumbai studio has grown from an intimate bespoke boutique into one of India’s most celebrated wedding management houses. We have brought to life over 250 royal palace weddings in Rajasthan, sun-drenched coastal vows in Goa, and cosmopolitan ballroom soirees across Mumbai, Delhi, and international shores.
